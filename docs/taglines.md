# Papercut taglines

Tagline brainstorm for Papercut, a cozy papercraft puzzle game set in the narrative world of the paper buddy.

Target sentiments:

- This is relaxing.
- This is challenging, fun, and rewarding.
- You are discovering a world.
- You are customizing your home and your character.

Starred entries (★) are current favorites.

## Current direction: "Tricky to cut, satisfying to unfold"

| Tagline | Category | Comments |
